Using Rushes

How do I make a video?

Tap Pick clips, choose the clips from your day, and wait for the cut. Watch it, fix any caption, pick a length and a style, then tap Save to Photos.

Why does it need my Photos?

To read the clips you pick and to save the finished video. You can give Rushes access to only the clips you choose. It never uploads them.

Why does the cut take a while?

Your phone is transcribing every clip and rendering the video itself. Nothing is sent to a server to speed it up. Keep the app open while it works; a long day takes a few minutes.

A caption has the wrong word.

Tap the word and type the right one. It changes everywhere that word appears in the video.

Rushes skipped a clip I wanted.

Open the skipped list under the video and tap the clip to put it back. The video recuts.

I deleted a clip from Photos.

Rushes notices the next time you open that day and recuts it without the missing clip.

What phones does it run on?

iPhone 15 Pro and newer, and iPads with an M-series or A17 Pro chip, on iOS 26 or later.

Purchases

What's free?

Everything up to saving. Pick clips, watch the cut, fix captions, change the length and style. Saving the video to Photos needs the monthly plan ($4.99 a month) or the one-time purchase ($39.99).

I paid but the app says I haven't.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple account you bought with.

How do I cancel?

The monthly plan is billed by Apple. Cancel it in iOS Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions. The one-time purchase has nothing to cancel.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Do my clips leave my phone?

No. Rushes has no server. The Privacy Policy has the details.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Rushes keeps nothing anywhere else. Your clips and saved videos in Photos are yours and stay where they are.

Something broke

Email admin@useaidailydose.com with what you did, what happened, and your phone model. A screen recording helps a lot.
