Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Rushes is made by AI Daily Dose LLC. This policy is short because the app is: everything Rushes does happens on your device. There is no Rushes server, no account and no analytics.

1. What stays on your device

  • Your clips. Rushes reads the clips you pick from your Photos library to cut and caption them. It never copies them anywhere else and never uploads them. You can give it access to only the clips you choose.
  • What you said. Your device transcribes the speech in your clips using Apple's on-device speech recognition. The transcript, your caption edits, your style and length choices, and the list of clips in each day are stored only in the app on your device.
  • The finished video. Your device renders it and saves it to your Photos library when you ask. We never receive it.

Delete the app and all of this is gone. Deleting a day in the app removes its transcript and edits; your original clips and any saved videos in Photos are yours and are not affected.

2. What is sent anywhere

Nothing by Rushes. The app does not connect to any server we run, and it includes no analytics, crash reporting or advertising code.

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple tells the app whether you have an active purchase; we do not see your name, email, or payment details. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that transaction.

3. Permissions

  • Photos. To read the clips you pick and to save the finished video. Rushes works with limited Photos access.
  • Speech recognition. Runs on the device. Nothing is sent to Apple's servers for this.

You can change either permission at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Children

Rushes is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect any from anyone.

5. Changes

If a future version of Rushes ever sends anything off the device, this policy will say exactly what, and the app will ask you first.
