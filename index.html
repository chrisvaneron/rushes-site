Good to know.

Does Rushes post for me?

No. It saves the video to Photos. You post it wherever you like, with whatever caption you like.

What does it run on?

iPhone 15 Pro and newer, and iPads with an M-series or A17 Pro chip, on iOS 26 or later. The cut runs on the phone, so it needs a phone that can do it.

What languages does it caption?

The languages your iPhone can transcribe on the device. English is the one we test most.

Do my clips leave my phone?

No. There is no server to send them to. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Can I change what it picked?

Yes. Put a skipped clip back, change the length, fix any word, pick another caption style, and the video updates.

What if I delete a clip from Photos?

Rushes notices and recuts the day without it.
